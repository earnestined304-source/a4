Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Comprehensive information on how SockEssentials safeguards customer records, payment details, and privacy rights.

1. Comprehensive Privacy & Data Protection Commitment

At SockEssentials Studio ('SockEssentials', 'we', 'our', or 'us'), protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of our customers, website visitors, and community members is of paramount importance. This Privacy Policy sets forth our detailed policies regarding the collection, storage, processing, sharing, and protection of personal data gathered through our website, mobile interfaces, customer service channels, and physical studio headquarters at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States.

By browsing our website, creating an account, engaging with our customer desk, or purchasing our ergonomic hosiery products, you acknowledge and agree to the data management practices described in this comprehensive document.

2. Detailed Information We Collect

We collect personal information through direct interactions, automated tracking technologies, and verified third-party integrations to deliver a seamless and personalized customer experience:

  • Direct Customer Identifiers: Legal full name, shipping and billing addresses, email address, telephone number, and account login credentials.
  • Commercial & Transactional Records: Details of sock styles purchased, sizing preferences (e.g., Small, Medium, Large, Extra Large), shoe fit specifications, payment confirmation tokens, order history, and customer service correspondence transcripts.
  • Technical & Browsing Analytics: Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type, operating system version, device identifiers, referring URL trajectories, pages viewed, time spent per product page, and session clickstream data gathered via Google Tag Manager (Tag ID: G-0LY0HY7L01).
  • Fit Survey & Preference Responses: Customer responses to our interactive Sock Fiber & Fit Navigator, activity preferences (e.g., trail hiking, marathon running, daily work wear), and optional textile feedback.

3. How We Process and Utilize Your Personal Data

SockEssentials utilizes collected customer data strictly for legitimate commercial and customer-support objectives, including:

  • Fulfilling, packaging, and dispatching orders with tracked logistics carriers.
  • Processing secure payment authorizations and managing our 100-Day Wash & Wear Comfort Guarantee.
  • Providing responsive customer support, resolving sizing inquiries, and issuing care guidance for merino wool and organic cotton products.
  • Optimizing website navigation speed, diagnosing technical anomalies, and refining our digital hosiery fit algorithms.
  • Sending essential operational notifications, such as order confirmations, tracking alerts, and policy updates.

We maintain a strict institutional policy: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or lease your personal information to third-party data brokers or external advertisers under any circumstances.

4. Data Sharing with Trusted Service Providers

We disclose limited personal data only to verified third-party service providers who are contractually bound to maintain strict confidentiality and data protection standards:

  • Logistics & Delivery Carriers: Providing shipping addresses and recipient telephone numbers to reliable national and international postal couriers.
  • Encrypted Payment Gateways: Transmitting tokenized transaction details directly to PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processors. We never store raw credit card numbers on our local servers.
  • Analytics & Infrastructure Hosts: Cloud server hosting and aggregate analytics providers operating in secure, encrypted data center environments.

5. Data Retention Schedules

We retain personal customer information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the operational purposes for which it was collected, comply with legal accounting regulations, resolve disputes, and enforce our commercial agreements. Inactive account records and completed transaction logs are routinely sanitized and purged according to our institutional retention schedules.

6. Security Architecture & Safeguards

SockEssentials implements multi-layered administrative, physical, and technical safeguards designed to protect personal data against accidental loss, unauthorized access, destruction, alteration, or disclosure. These measures include 256-bit Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) transport encryption, internal role-based access controls, firewalled cloud architecture, and regular vulnerability scanning.

7. Consumer Rights under GDPR and CCPA

Depending on your geographic residence, you may hold specific statutory rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • Right to Access: Requesting a copy of the specific pieces of personal information we maintain regarding your account.
  • Right to Rectification: Requesting the correction of inaccurate or incomplete contact and delivery details.
  • Right to Erasure ('Right to be Forgotten'): Requesting the permanent deletion of your personal account records, subject to statutory recordkeeping requirements.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Requesting limits on how we process your personal data in certain contested circumstances.

8. Children's Online Privacy Protection

Our website and products are intended for general consumer audiences. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal data from individuals under the age of sixteen without verified parental consent. If we become aware that a child under sixteen has provided personal information, we promptly delete such data from our databases.

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data rights, please contact our Data Privacy Officer via mail or telephone:
