Cookie Transparency & Tracking Policy

Granular explanation of essential cookies, analytics metrics, and how to manage your privacy settings.

1. What Are Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Cookies are small text files placed upon your computer, tablet, or mobile device by websites that you visit. They are widely utilized to ensure web platforms operate efficiently, remember user preferences across visits (such as your chosen light or dark theme), enhance checkout security, and provide aggregate analytical insights to website administrators.

This Cookie Policy explains how SockEssentials Studio ('SockEssentials', 'we', 'us') uses cookies and similar technologies on our website, and outlines the choices available to you to manage your privacy preferences.

2. Categories of Cookies We Deploy

We classify the cookies utilized on our platform into four distinct functional tiers:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: These cookies are essential for the technical operation of our website. They enable core functions such as secure shopping cart memory, tokenized session security, page navigation, and responsive layout rendering. The website cannot function properly without these cookies.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: We utilize Google Analytics (Google Tag ID: G-0LY0HY7L01) to collect anonymous, aggregate information about how visitors navigate our website, which sock articles are most read, and how visitors move through our Sock Fiber & Fit Navigator. This data helps us identify technical bottlenecks and refine user experience. These cookies do not store direct personal identifiers.
  • Functionality & Preference Cookies: These cookies enable our website to remember choices you have made, such as your selected visual theme (Light Mode vs. Dark Theme via local storage) and recently viewed hosiery styles, providing a tailored and smooth browsing experience.
  • Security & Fraud Prevention Cookies: Utilized during checkout interactions to identify suspicious traffic patterns, protect user accounts against automated credential attacks, and safeguard payment submissions.

4. How to Control and Manage Your Cookie Preferences

You have the absolute right to decide whether to accept or reject non-essential cookies. You can exercise your preferences at any time by configuring your web browser settings:

  • Browser Settings: Most modern web browsers allow you to view, manage, delete, and block cookies through their privacy settings. Please note that if you disable strictly necessary cookies, certain features of our interactive fit studio and shopping bag may not operate correctly.
  • Google Analytics Opt-Out: To prevent your aggregate browsing data from being collected by Google Analytics across all websites, you may install the official Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on provided by Google.

5. Updates to This Cookie Policy

SockEssentials may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect operational changes, new browser standards, or evolving regulatory requirements. Any modifications will become effective immediately upon posting to this page, with the revision date updated accordingly.
