How It Started

Built after one too many jackets fell apart at the seams

Jacket Heritage started with a specific frustration: a jacket marketed as heritage workwear that split at the shoulder seam within a season of normal wear. We started sourcing raw denim and waxed canvas ourselves, testing construction against real daily use before committing to a single design.

Every jacket since has been built around that same standard.
