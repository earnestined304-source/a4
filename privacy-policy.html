Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains in detail how Jacket Heritage ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information gathered through this website, www.jacketheritage.com, and through direct contact with our workshop. We are a small, independently operated workshop, and this policy applies only to this site and our own direct communications with customers — it does not extend to any third-party site we may link to.

Information We Collect

We collect information in a few limited ways. When you use the contact form on this site, we collect whatever you choose to provide — typically your name, email address, and the content of your message, which may include sizing preferences, order questions, or general inquiries. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect payment or financial information through this website.

Like most websites, we also use basic analytics tools that may log general technical information such as browser type, approximate location at a city or region level, device type, and which pages were visited. This information is aggregated and is not used to identify you personally.

How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through our contact form is used solely to respond to your specific inquiry — whether that's a question about a jacket's fit, fabric, availability, or an order you'd like to place. We do not use this information for unrelated marketing purposes unless you explicitly ask to be added to any communication list we may offer, and we never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Analytics and the Google Tag

This site uses Google's tagging service (gtag.js) to understand overall site traffic and how visitors generally use the site — for example, which pages are most visited or how long a typical session lasts. This data is viewed in aggregate and is not used to build individual profiles of specific visitors. You can control or limit this kind of tracking through your own browser settings, including disabling cookies or using browser extensions designed for this purpose.

Cookies

This site uses a limited number of cookies, primarily to support basic site functionality and the aggregate analytics described above. We do not use cookies to serve targeted third-party advertising. For more detail on the specific types of cookies used, please see our separate Cookie Policy.

Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry, complete any related order, and maintain basic business records consistent with standard bookkeeping practices. If you would like us to delete information you've previously submitted, you may request this using the contact details below, and we will honor reasonable requests promptly.

How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable, good-faith steps to protect the information you share with us, including limiting who on our small team has access to customer inquiries and using standard website security practices. That said, no method of transmission over the internet is entirely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security for information submitted through this website.

Your Choices

You are never required to provide any information through this website beyond what's needed to respond to a specific inquiry. You may also ask us at any time what information we hold about you, request a correction, or request deletion, using the contact information below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal or operational reasons. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision, and we encourage you to review it occasionally if you have ongoing questions about how your information is handled.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please reach out to us directly at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or by phone at +1-888-777-5845.
