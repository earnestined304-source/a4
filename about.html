Our Story

Made because a jacket shouldn't split at the shoulder

We're a small, independent workshop — not a large manufacturer. Here's the plain version of how we got here.

Where It Started

A "heritage" jacket that split within a season

Jacket Heritage started with a specific frustration: a jacket marketed as heritage workwear that split at the shoulder seam within a season of normal wear. We began sourcing raw denim and waxed canvas ourselves, testing construction against real daily use before committing to a single design.

The first Jacket Heritage jacket was built entirely around what survived that testing — and every jacket since has followed the same process.

How We Work

A few habits we hold to on every jacket

01

Fabric tested, not just chosen

Every denim and canvas weight is worn and washed repeatedly before it becomes a product.

02

Seams reinforced, not just stitched

Stress points get extra reinforcement so jackets hold up through real daily wear.

03

Small batches, checked by hand

Every batch gets a hands-on quality check before it ships.
